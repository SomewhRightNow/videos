# Critique — draft-v5

1. **18.0–20.4 s · the window diagram says the opposite of the narration.** While the voice says the window was "turned away from the mountain", a WINDOW box is drawn and then a PELÉE triangle appears right beside it, at the same height, on the side the box faces. A first-time viewer reads this as "the window faces the volcano". This is the one fact the whole film depends on. The box also floats on a translucent grey band over the vine-covered wall, well above the real cell opening, so the graphic looks unfinished.
   *Smallest fix:* move the PELÉE triangle behind the window (to the far side of the cell, with the cell wall between them), or add an arrow from the window pointing away from the triangle. Anchor the box on the real opening above the door and drop the grey band.

2. **24.0–25.1 s · the circus line plays over a film still labelled "MÉLIÈS · STAGED ERUPTION · 1902".** The voice says "Later he tours with a circus", but the picture is a staged volcano from a 1902 film. That is the wrong subject and the wrong medium, and it brings the eruption back after we have already moved on to the man. The tag "Méliès" means nothing to most viewers.
   *Smallest fix:* replace that shot with a circus or Barnum & Bailey poster or tent image, or give the "BARNUM & BAILEY · LATER" portrait its 1.5 s now and remove the Méliès shot.

3. **21–23 s and 26–30 s · the same portrait twice, and the film ends on 4 s of a still.** The framed Sylbaris photo plays under "Burned…" and then the identical picture comes back for the ending, where it holds from about 26 s to 30 s with only the title typing on. It reads as a repeat and the energy dies at the end. The last image never pays off the opening "one minute left": there is no return to the city or the mountain.
   *Smallest fix:* use a different image for one of the two (for example a burn or rescue shot, or the ruins, under "Burned…"), and for the end give the portrait a slow push-in, or cut to the ruined city with the title over it around 28 s.
