May 8th, 1902. Just before eight a.m.
Saint-Pierre, a city of twenty-eight thousand, has one minute left.
Mount Pelée sends a burning cloud down onto the town.
Almost no one survives.
One man does, because he is in jail.
Ludger Sylbaris sits in a stone cell, its window turned away from the mountain.
Burned, he calls out for four days before anyone hears him.
Later he tours with a circus, as the man who lived through Doomsday.
