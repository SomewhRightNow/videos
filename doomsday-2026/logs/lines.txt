Today, Friday the thirteenth of November, is doomsday. According to science.
In nineteen sixty, Heinz von Foerster published a formula in the journal Science.
It said the world's population would hit infinity, on Friday, November thirteenth, twenty twenty-six.
Back then, there were under three billion of us. Today, over eight billion.
But population growth slowed down, and the formula broke.
