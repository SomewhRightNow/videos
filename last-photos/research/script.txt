These are some of the last photos taken before disaster. Wait for the last one.
April eleventh, nineteen twelve. A priest steps off the Titanic in Ireland and takes this photo. Four days later, the ship was at the bottom of the Atlantic.
May sixth, nineteen thirty-seven. The Hindenburg flies over New York, on its way to land. Hours later, it burst into flames.
May seventeenth, nineteen eighty. Mount Saint Helens, one day before it exploded. A young volcanologist was watching from a nearby ridge. His last words: Vancouver, Vancouver, this is it.
January twenty-eighth, nineteen eighty-six. Challenger lifts off with seven astronauts on board. Seventy-three seconds later, it broke apart.
And this one. Columbia's crew, floating in space. This film was found in the wreckage, after the shuttle broke apart on its way home.
