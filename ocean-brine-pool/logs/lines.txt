At the bottom of the Gulf of Mexico, there is a lake. Under the sea.
Ocean Mysteries, number three. The Jacuzzi of Despair.
It sits about a kilometre down. It has a shoreline, and even ripples on its surface.
The water inside is four to five times saltier than the ocean around it, and much warmer.
It is so heavy that it does not mix. It just lies on the seafloor, like a lake.
There is almost no oxygen in it. Methane bubbles up from below.
Fish and crabs that wander in often never come out. Their bodies lie preserved on the bottom.
Only mussels live along its edge, feeding on that methane.
