These animals look fake. They're not. Wait for the last one.
The shoebill. Five feet tall, and it can stand perfectly still for hours, waiting to strike.
The axolotl. If it loses a leg, it grows a new one. It can even repair parts of its heart and brain.
The aye-aye. It taps on trees with one long, bony finger, listens for insects inside, then hooks them out.
The saiga antelope. That huge nose filters dust in summer, and warms freezing air in winter.
The glass frog. Its belly is see-through. You can watch its heart beating.
And the mantis shrimp. It punches with the speed of a bullet, hard enough to crack aquarium glass.
