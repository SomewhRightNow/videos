7. Félix and María Williams wanted a better life, so they headed north, across the Sahara.
8. Their son Iñaki would later say his parents crossed that desert barefoot.
9. At the end of it was Melilla, a small piece of Spain on the African coast, behind a border fence.

[Harita: Melilla -> Bilbao]

15. Eight years later, in Pamplona, came his little brother. Nico.
