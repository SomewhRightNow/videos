Five people who should not be alive. Number one fell ten kilometres, with no parachute.
Five. Harrison Okene, 2013. His tugboat sank thirty metres to the sea floor. He breathed from an air pocket for almost three days.
Four. Juliane Koepcke, 1971. Her plane broke apart over the Amazon. She fell three kilometres in her seat, then walked out of the jungle.
Three. José Alvarenga. Lost at sea in a small fishing boat for four hundred and thirty-eight days.
Two. Tsutomu Yamaguchi, 1945. He survived the atomic bomb in Hiroshima. Then he went home, to Nagasaki.
And number one. Vesna Vulović, 1972. Her plane exploded at ten thousand metres. She fell all the way down, and lived.
