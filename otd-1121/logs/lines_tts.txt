On this day in two thousand seven, England needed only a draw against Croatia at Wembley to reach Euro two thousand eight.
Eight minutes in, Scott Carson let Niko KranyChar's shot slip past him.
Six minutes later, Ivica Oleech made it two-nil.
Frank Lampard scored from the spot, and Peter Crouch made it two-all.
Then, with thirteen minutes left, Mladen Petrich won it from distance.
England were out. Steve McClaren was sacked the next day.
