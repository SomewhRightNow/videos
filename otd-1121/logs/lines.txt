On this day in 2007, England needed only a draw against Croatia at Wembley to reach Euro 2008.
Eight minutes in, Scott Carson let Niko Kranjčar's shot slip past him.
Six minutes later, Ivica Olić made it 2–0.
Frank Lampard scored from the spot, and Peter Crouch made it 2–2.
Then, with thirteen minutes left, Mladen Petrić won it from distance.
England were out. Steve McClaren was sacked the next day.
