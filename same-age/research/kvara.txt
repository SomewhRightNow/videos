'''Khvicha Kvaratskhelia''' ({{post-nominals|post-noms=[[Order of Honor (Georgia)|Order of Honor]]}}) ({{lang-ka|ხვიჩა კვარაცხელია}}; {{IPA|ka|'χʷitʃʰa kʼʷara'tsʰχelia|pron}}; born 12 February 2001)<ref name=":0">{{cite news |last=Morse |first=Ben |title=Khvicha Kvaratskhelia: Napoli's Georgian sensation billed 'Kvaradona' |url=https://edition.cnn.com/2022/11/01/football/khvicha-kvaratskhelia-napoli-georgia-spt-intl/index.html |publisher=CNN |access-date=19 August 2023 |date=1 November 2022 |quote='Kvara changed life in Georgia [...]'}}</ref> is a Georgian professional [[association football|footballer]] who plays as a [[left winger]] for [[Ligue 1]] club [[Paris Saint-Germain FC|Paris Saint-Germain]] and [[Captain (association football)|captains]] the [[Georgia national football team|Georgia national team]]. He is regarded as one of the best players in the world and the best Georgian player of all time.<ref>{{Cite web |last=O'Hanlon |first=Ryan |date=28 August 2026 |title=10 Best: Ranking the top soccer players in the world right now |url=https://www.espn.co.uk/football/story/_/id/49742377/10-best-ranking-top-soccer-players-world-right-now |access-date=23 September 2023 |website=ESPN}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Swan |first=Rob |last2=Malyan |first2=Christy |date=30 August 2026 |title=The 30 best footballers in the world right now - ranked in order |url=https://www.givemesport.com/best-football-players-in-the-world/ |access-date=23 September 2026 |website=GiveMeSport |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Christenson |first=Marcus |last2=Duncan |first2=Pamela |last3=Bloor |first3=Steven |last4=Blight |first4=Garry |date=19 December 2025 |title=The 100 best male footballers in the world 2025 |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/ng-interactive/2025/dec/16/the-100-best-male-footballers-in-the-world-2025 |access-date=23 September 2026 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

Kvaratskhelia started his senior career playing for [[FC Dinamo Tbilisi|Dinamo Tbilisi]] at age 16, before departing to [[FC Rustavi|Rustavi]] where he spent half a season. During his combined three-year tenure at [[FC Lokomotiv Moscow|Lokomotiv Moscow]] and [[FC Rubin Kazan|Rubin Kazan]], Kvaratskhelia won two consecutive [[Russian Premier League]] Best Young Player awards.

Following a short spell at [[FC Dinamo Batumi|Dinamo Batumi]], Kvaratskhelia signed for Italian side [[SSC Napoli|Napoli]] in 2022, and in his debut season, he helped the club win their first [[2022–23 Serie A|Serie A title]]  since 1990, being named [[Serie A Awards|Serie A's Most Valuable Player]] and the [[2022–23 UEFA Champions League|Champions League's Young Player of the Season]].<ref>{{cite web |date=23 June 2023 |title=კვარაცხელიას სუპერგოლი იტალიაში სეზონის საუკეთესოდ დაასახელეს |trans-title=Kvaratskhelia's super goal was named the best of the season in Italy. |url=https://europop.ge/article/54140-kvaratskhelias-supergoli-italiashi-sezonis-sauketesod-daasakheles |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231002210732/https://europop.ge/article/54140-kvaratskhelias-supergoli-italiashi-sezonis-sauketesod-daasakheles |archive-date=2 October 2023 |access-date=23 June 2023 |work=europop |publisher=Europop.ge |language=ka}}</ref> He joined French side Paris Saint-Germain for €70 million in January 2025. He immediately became a focal player, as the club won a [[Treble (association football)#Continental trebles|continental treble]] in his first season there, scoring in the [[2025 UEFA Champions League final|Champions League final]].<ref>{{cite web |date=17 January 2025 |title=Why Paris Saint-Germain signed Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and didn't want anyone else |url=https://www.espn.com/soccer/story/_/id/43454851/why-paris-saint-germain-signed-khvicha-kvaratskhelia |access-date=19 January 2025 |website=ESPN}}</ref> He won a second Champions League title [[2025–26 UEFA Champions League|the following season]] and was named the tournament's [[UEFA Champions League#Player of the Season|best player]].<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20260531-kvaratskhelia-named-champions-league-player-of-the-season |title=Kvaratskhelia named Champions League player of the season |publisher=France 24 |date=31 May 2026 }}</ref>

Kvaratskhelia established himself for Georgia in all national youth teams, becoming a key player under different managers. He then became an integral part of the senior team after making his debut in 2019, and helped Georgia qualify for [[UEFA Euro 2024]], the country's first ever major international tournament.<ref name=":0" /><ref name="auto">{{cite news |date=7 September 2022 |title=Kvaradona! Meet Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, the new Napoli star who is gunning for Liverpool in the Champions League |url=https://www.goal.com/en/news/kvaradona-meet-khvicha-kvaratskhelia-the-new-napoli-star-gunning-for-liverpool-in-the-champions-league/bltf6dee7c937cb4cfe |access-date=16 September 2023 |website=Goal.com}}</ref><ref name="auto1">{{cite web |date=7 April 2023 |title=Kaladze reveals three favourite Milan memories and weighs in on Leao vs. Kvaratskhelia debate |url=https://sempremilan.com/kaladze-three-milan-memories-leao-kvaratskhelia-napoli |access-date=16 September 2023 |website=SempreMilan}}</ref>

== Club career ==
=== Early career ===
Coming through the youth system, Kvaratskhelia began his senior career at [[FC Dinamo Tbilisi|Dinamo Tbilisi]] in 2017, making his senior debut against [[FC Kolkheti-1913 Poti|Kolkheti-1913]], coming on as a 62nd-minute substitute in a 1–1 draw on 29 September 2017. In total, Kvaratskhelia made five appearances in all competitions for Dinamo Tbilisi, scoring his first goal in a 1–0 win away to [[FC Shukura Kobuleti|Shukura Kobuleti]] on 19 November 2017.<ref name="soccerway">{{Soccerway|new_id=kvaratskhelia-khvicha/bNuGt0hE|id=khvicha-kvaratskhelia/463156}}</ref>

In March 2018, Kvaratskhelia left Dinamo Tbilisi as a result of a contract dispute and subsequently signed for [[FC Rustavi|Rustavi]] on a free transfer.<ref>{{cite web |date=14 October 2020 |title=ბავშვი, რომელიც ყველას ატყუებს - 10 წლის კვარაცხელია ძმის კვალზე |trans-title=The child who deceives everyone – 10-year-old Kvaratskhelia following in his brother's footsteps. |url=https://adjarasport.com/fekhburti/posts/bavshvi-romelits-qvelas-atquebs-10-tslis-kvaratskhelia-dzmis-kvalze-480492 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220718192533/https://adjarasport.com/fekhburti/posts/bavshvi-romelits-qvelas-atquebs-10-tslis-kvaratskhelia-dzmis-kvalze-480492 |archive-date=18 July 2022 |access-date=18 July 2022 |website=Adjara Sport |language=ka}}</ref> In April 2018, Kvaratskhelia was the subject of interest from German champions [[FC Bayern Munich|Bayern Munich]] and was in attendance for Bayern's 0–0 draw against [[Sevilla FC|Sevilla]] in the [[UEFA Champions League]] quarter-final.<ref>{{cite web |date=7 August 2018 |title=17 წლის ქართველი სუპერტალანტი - მიუნხენის ბაიერნს მოსკოვის ლოკომოტივი ეჭიდავება |trans-title=The 17-year-old Georgian super talent – Munich's Bayern is competing with Moscow's Lokomotiv. |url=https://europop.ge/article/5640-17-clis-kartveli-supertalanti-miunkhenis-baierns-moskovis-lokomotivi-echidaveba |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230317210037/https://europop.ge/article/5640-17-clis-kartveli-supertalanti-miunkhenis-baierns-moskovis-lokomotivi-echidaveba |archive-date=17 March 2023 |access-date=17 March 2023 |website=Europop |language=ka}}</ref> During the [[2018 Erovnuli Liga]] season, Kvaratskhelia scored three goals across 18 appearances for Rustavi.<ref name="soccerway"/>

In 2018, ''[[The Guardian]]'' named Kvaratskhelia among 60 best young players worldwide.<ref name=":1">{{Cite news |last1=Christenson |first1=Marcus |last2=Powell |first2=Jim |last3=Blight |first3=Garry |last4=Hulley-Jones |first4=Frank |date=2018-10-10 |title=Next Generation 2018: 60 of the best young talents in world football |url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/ng-interactive/2018/oct/11/next-generation-2018-60-of-the-best-young-talents-in-world-football |access-date=2025-05-11 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref>

=== Lokomotiv Moscow ===
[[File:Khvicha Kvaratskhelia 2019.jpg|left|thumb|193x193px|Kvaratskhelia with [[FC Lokomotiv Moscow|Lokomotiv Moscow]] in 2019]]
On 15 February 2019, Kvaratskhelia joined [[Russian Premier League]] (RPL) club [[FC Lokomotiv Moscow|Lokomotiv Moscow]] on loan.<ref name="InKK">{{cite web |date=15 February 2019 |title=Хвича Кварацхелия – в "Локомотиве" |trans-title=Khvicha Kvaratskhelia – at "Lokomotiv" |url=https://www.fclm.ru/ru/publications/news/19535 |publisher=[[FC Lokomotiv Moscow]] |language=ru}}</ref> He made his league debut on 10 March as an 86th-minute substitute for [[Jefferson Farfán]] against [[FC Anzhi Makhachkala|Anzhi Makhachkala]].<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[Russian Premier League]]|url=https://premierliga.ru/match/match_12726.html|title=Anzhi v Lokomotiv game report|date=10 March 2019|language=ru|access-date=10 March 2019|archive-date=11 August 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200811031635/https://premierliga.ru/match/match_12726.html|url-status=dead}}</ref> On 1 July, Lokomotiv Moscow announced that Kvaratskhelia had left the club after his loan expired.<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[FC Lokomotiv Moscow]] |url=https://www.fclm.ru/ru/publications/news/20350|title=Кварацхелия покинул "Локомотив"|trans-title=Kvaratskhelia left Lokomotiv|date=1 July 2019|language=ru}}</ref> Following the loss of Kvaratskhelia, Lokomotiv Moscow manager [[Yuri Semin]] said he was very disappointed after failing to agree on a permanent transfer with Kvaratskhelia as Semin considered him extremely talented.<ref>{{cite web|publisher=Championat|url=https://www.championat.com/football/news-3793843-sjomin-iz-za-uhoda-kvarachelii-iz-lokomotiva-u-menja-potekli-sljozy.html|title=Сёмин: из-за ухода Кварацхелии из "Локомотива" у меня потекли слёзы|trans-title=Semin: I cried because Kvaratskhelia left Lokomotiv|date=1 July 2019|language=ru}}</ref>

=== Rubin Kazan ===
[[File:Khvicha Kvaratskhelia Rubin Kazan, 2019 (cropped).jpg|thumb|245x245px|Kvaratskhelia with [[FC Rubin Kazan|Rubin Kazan]] in 2019]]
On 6 July 2019, Kvaratskhelia signed a five-year deal with [[FC Rubin Kazan|Rubin Kazan]].<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[FC Rubin Kazan]]|url=https://rubin-kazan.ru/posts/hvicha-kvaratsheliya-stal-igrokom-rubina|title=ХВИЧА КВАРАЦХЕЛИЯ СТАЛ ИГРОКОМ "РУБИНА"|trans-title=Khvicha Kvaratskhelia became a Rubin player|date=6 July 2019|language=ru|access-date=8 July 2019|archive-date=8 July 2019|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190708153708/https://rubin-kazan.ru/posts/hvicha-kvaratsheliya-stal-igrokom-rubina|url-status=dead}}</ref> He played his first game on 15 July against his previous club Lokomotiv, coming on as a substitute in the second half and scoring the equalizer in a 1–1 draw. Kvaratskhelia was named as best player of the match by Rubin fans on the official site of the club.<ref>{{cite web |title=Кварацхелия признан болельщиками "Рубина" лучшим игроком матча с "Локомотивом" |trans-title=Kvaratskhelia was named the best player of the match against "Lokomotiv" by Rubin fans. |url=https://www.sovsport.ru/football/news/2:911320 |website=sovsport.ru (in Russian), 18 July 2019}}</ref>

Overall, his purchase was hailed by several Russian media outlets as the main transfer success for Rubin, who saw the player's market value increased five times in a single season between June 2019 and June 2020.<ref>{{cite web |title=10 лучших молодых игроков РПЛ, которые раскрылись в сезоне 2019/20 |trans-title=The 10 best young players of the Russian Premier League who emerged in the 2019/20 season. |url=https://www.championat.com/football/article-4095305-luchshie-molodye-futbolisty-rpl-kotorye-raskrylis-v-sezone-20192020.html |website=championat.com (in Russian), 31 July 2020}}</ref> Based on the votes among Rubin supporters, Kvaratskhelia won Player of the Month nomination four times in 2020–21, namely in August, September, October and April.<ref>{{cite news |title=ხვიჩა კვარაცხელია რუბინის თვის საუკეთესო მოთამაშე გახდა |url=https://www.silksport.ge/NewsPost/0/6670/1 |publisher=silksport.ge (in Georgian) |date=3 May 2021 |access-date=5 July 2022 |archive-date=6 July 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220706000549/https://www.silksport.ge/NewsPost/0/6670/1 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

In early 2021, ''[[L'Équipe]]'' published a list of 50 best players born in the 21st century, with Kvaratskhelia being the only RPL player to be included.<ref>{{cite web |date=12 February 2021 |title=Кварацхелия вошел в топ-50 молодых игроков в мире, первый — Фати |trans-title=Kvaratskhelia entered the top 50 young players in the world, with Fati in first place. |url=https://www.sport-express.ru/football/rfpl/news/kvaracheliya-voshel-v-top-50-molodyh-igrokov-v-mire-pervyy-fati-1756930/#! |website=sport-express.ru (in Russian), 12 February 2021}}</ref>

=== Dinamo Batumi ===
On 7 March 2022, [[FIFA]] announced that, due to the [[2022 Russian invasion of Ukraine|Russian invasion of Ukraine]], foreign players in Russia could unilaterally suspend their contracts until 30 June, and were allowed to sign with clubs outside of Russia until the same date.<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[FIFA]] |url=https://www.fifa.com/about-fifa/organisation/fifa-council/media-releases/fifa-adopts-temporary-employment-and-registration-rules-to-address-several|title=FIFA adopts temporary employment and registration rules to address several issues in relation to war in Ukraine|date=7 March 2022}}</ref> On 24 March 2022, Rubin Kazan announced that Kvaratskhelia's contract had been suspended.<ref>{{cite press release |title="РУБИН" И ХВИЧА ДОГОВОРИЛИСЬ О ПРИОСТАНОВКЕ КОНТРАКТА |date=24 March 2022 |publisher=[[FC Rubin Kazan]] |url=https://rubin-kazan.ru/posts/rubin-i-hvicha-dogovorilis-o-priostanovke-kontrakta |language=ru |access-date=24 March 2022 |url-status=dead |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231210170228/https://www.rubin-kazan.ru/posts/rubin-i-hvicha-dogovorilis-o-priostanovke-kontrakta |archive-date=10 December 2023 |trans-title="Rubin" and Kvaratskhelia have agreed to suspend the contract.}}</ref> On the same day, he joined [[FC Dinamo Batumi|Dinamo Batumi]] of Georgia.<ref>{{cite web|publisher=[[FC Dinamo Batumi|Dinamo Batumi]] |url=https://www.facebook.com/DinamoBatumi1923/posts/3011126885766758|title=Say what?|date=24 March 2022|access-date=24 March 2022}}</ref>

Kvaratskhelia took part in 11 league matches with Dinamo Batumi, scoring eight times and providing two assists. [[Erovnuli Liga]] named him the best player of the second round of the season, which coincided with the period between April and July.<ref>{{cite web |title=ელიგის მეორე წრეში საუკეთესო კვარაცხელიაა |url=https://1tv.ge/news/eligis-meore-wreshi-sauketeso-kvarackheliaa-1tvsport/ |website=1tv.ge (in Georgian) |date=23 June 2022}}</ref>

== Personal life ==
Khvicha is the son of [[Badri Kvaratskhelia]], a Georgian footballer who played for several clubs, including [[FC Dinamo Tbilisi]], and represented the [[Azerbaijani national football team]]. He has two brothers, including younger brother [[Tornike Kvaratskhelia|Tornike]] (born 2010), who is also a footballer.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://en.nogomania.com/read/Another-Kvaratskhelia-rising-16-year-old-Tornike-goes-viral-with-stunning-talent |title=Another Kvaratskhelia rising: 16-year-old Tornike goes viral with stunning talent |website=nogomania.com |date=9 April 2026 }}</ref>

His family is originally from [[Samegrelo]] and he grew up speaking [[Mingrelian language|Mingrelian]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Whitehead |first=Jacob |date=2026-05-29 |title=Khvicha Kvaratskhelia: From a boy who watched apples grow to a man seeking his second Champions League |url=https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7317490/2026/05/29/psg-champions-league-final-khvicha-kvaratskhelia/ |access-date=2026-06-24 |work=The New York Times |language=en-US |issn=0362-4331}}</ref>

In August 2022, Kvaratskhelia revealed in an interview that he had been dating Nitsa Tavadze, a Georgian medical university student, for one year. Their marriage ceremony was held at [[Mtskheta]]'s [[Samtavro Monastery|Samtavro monastery]] in October 2023.<ref>{{cite web| url= 
https://fortuna.ge/fortuna/post/foto-video-ulamazesi-qartuli-ojakhi-kadrebi-khvicha-kvarackhelias-da-nica-tavadzis-jvrisweridan|title=ფოტო, ვიდეო: "ულამაზესი, ქართული ოჯახი"|website=fortuna.ge|date=16 October 2023|language=ka}}</ref>

Kvaratskhelia's son Damiane was born on 21 August 2024 in [[Tbilisi]].<ref>{{cite web |date=21 August 2024 |title="მშობიარობას "კვარაც" დაესწრო..." |trans-title="Kvara attended the childbirth..." |url=https://www.ambebi.ge/article/314224-mshobiarobas-kvarac-daescro-potoebi-chachava/ |access-date=22 August 2024 |website=ambebi.ge (in Georgian)}}</ref>
