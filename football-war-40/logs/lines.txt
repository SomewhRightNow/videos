In nineteen sixty-nine, a football match started a war.
Honduras versus El Salvador, for one World Cup ticket.
Three hundred thousand Salvadorans lived in Honduras.
Then Honduras started forcing them out.
In Tegucigalpa, Honduras won one-nil.
In San Salvador, El Salvador won three-nil.
Playoff in Mexico City: El Salvador won three-two.
That day, El Salvador cut all ties.
Weeks later, its army crossed the border.
Old World War Two fighters fought the last propeller dogfights in history.
The war lasted one hundred hours. Thousands died.
At the World Cup, El Salvador lost all three games, without a single goal.
