=== DESCRIPTION (EN) ===
He was supposed to be the next Pelé. A Copa Libertadores at 19. MSN with Messi and Suárez. La Remontada. A €222 million world-record transfer. Then the injuries came — and in July 2026, at the same stadium where he scored his first goal for Brazil, it ended in tears.

So what happened to Neymar? The injuries? Leaving Messi? Or did we simply expect too much? Tell us in ONE word in the comments 👇
