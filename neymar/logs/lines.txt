July, twenty twenty-six. MetLife Stadium, New Jersey.
Brazil are losing to Norway in the World Cup round of sixteen. Erling Haaland has scored twice.
In stoppage time, Neymar scores a penalty. It changes nothing.
At the final whistle, he falls to the ground in tears.
"I tried, I tried. Now, it's over. I started here. I finished here."
Sixteen years earlier, in this same stadium, he scored his first goal for Brazil.
So how did the boy who was supposed to be the next Pelé end up here?
This is what happened to Neymar.
Santos, Brazil. The club where Pelé became a legend. Neymar made his debut there at seventeen.
At nineteen, he led Santos to the Copa Libertadores, the biggest trophy in South America.
Europe's biggest clubs were already fighting for him.
In twenty thirteen, he joined Barcelona, next to Lionel Messi.
With Luis Suarez, they became MSN. In one season, the three of them scored one hundred and twenty-two goals.
In twenty fifteen, in Berlin, Neymar scored in the Champions League final. Barcelona won the treble.
Then came the night that made him a legend.
March, twenty seventeen. Barcelona had lost four-nil in Paris. At home, they needed a miracle.
In the eighty-eighth minute, they still needed three goals. Neymar scored two, and set up the last one in the ninety-fifth minute.
Six-one. They called it La Remontada. Many still call it the greatest comeback in football history.
He had already finished third in the Ballon d'Or. Many believed the next one would be his.
But at Barcelona, he would always be second to Messi.
August, twenty seventeen. Paris Saint-Germain paid his release clause. Two hundred and twenty-two million euros.
It was the most expensive transfer football had ever seen.
In Paris, he would finally be the main man. He would win the Champions League.
It never happened.
February, twenty eighteen. A broken bone in his right foot. He missed the second leg against Real Madrid. PSG went out.
January, twenty nineteen. The same foot again. He missed the tie against Manchester United. PSG went out again.
In twenty twenty, he finally reached the final. PSG lost one-nil to Bayern Munich.
Five years later, PSG won the Champions League for the first time. Neymar was already gone.
In twenty twenty-three, he moved to Saudi Arabia. Al Hilal paid around ninety million euros.
Two months later, playing for Brazil against Uruguay, his knee gave way. A torn ACL.
In a year and a half at Al Hilal, he played just seven games.
In twenty twenty-five, he went home to Santos, to get ready for one last World Cup.
Then his calf gave way. At the World Cup, he played in only two of Brazil's five games.
Today, Neymar is thirty-four.
This season at Santos, he has been shown more cards than he has scored goals.
Last month, he was sent off against Bahia, for covering his mouth while arguing with an opponent.
His father has just been named the club's chief executive. And his contract ends on the thirty-first of December.
But don't forget. He is Brazil's all-time top scorer. Eighty goals. Three more than Pelé.
Champions League. Copa Libertadores. Olympic gold.
So what happened to Neymar? The injuries? Leaving Messi? Or did we simply expect too much?
