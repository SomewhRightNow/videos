In 1997, the ocean made a sound no one could explain.
Ocean Mysteries, number one. The Bloop.
Underwater microphones, built to listen for submarines, picked it up in the Pacific.
Sensors more than three thousand kilometres apart heard the same sound.
It was so loud, people said only a giant creature could make it. Something bigger than a blue whale.
For years, nobody had an answer.
Then scientists listened near Antarctica, and heard the same kind of sound.
It was ice. An iceberg, cracking and breaking away from a glacier.
The ocean's monster was frozen water. Comment if you believed the monster, and follow for number two.
