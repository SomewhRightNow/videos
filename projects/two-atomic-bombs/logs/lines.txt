This man survived not one, but two atomic bombs.
His name was Tsutomu Yamaguchi. Part one.
In the summer of 1945, he was an engineer for Mitsubishi, sent to Hiroshima for three months of work.
August the sixth was supposed to be his last day there. On his way to the shipyard, he saw a plane high in the sky.
Then came a flash, brighter than the sun.
He was about three kilometres away. The blast burst his eardrums and burned the left side of his body.
He spent the night in a shelter. The next day, he caught a train home.
Home was Nagasaki.
On August the ninth, covered in bandages, he went back to work and told his boss what one bomb had done. His boss did not believe him.
