Facts: brine pool in the Gulf of Mexico, about 1,000 m deep (figures vary), nicknamed the 'Jacuzzi of Despair' by the E/V Nautilus team; brine 4 to 5 times saltier and much warmer than the sea around it; little or no oxygen; methane; dead animals preserved inside; mussels on its rim (study: Oceanography, 2016). Photos show Gulf of Mexico brine pools and seeps photographed by NOAA.
