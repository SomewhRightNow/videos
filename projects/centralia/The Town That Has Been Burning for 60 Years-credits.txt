Facts: mine fire since 1962 (cause debated); 1981 sinkhole: Todd Domboski, 12, ~150 ft deep, pulled out by his cousin, 14; ~1,000 residents in 1980;
relocation buyout and eminent domain (1992); ZIP 17927 discontinued 2002; 5 residents in the 2020 census; enough coal to burn ~250 more years (estimates vary).
