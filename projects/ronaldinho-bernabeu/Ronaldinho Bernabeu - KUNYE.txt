The Day Real Madrid Fans Applauded Ronaldinho 👏 #shorts

=== AÇIKLAMA (EN) ===
19 November 2005. Real Madrid 0-3 Barcelona at the Santiago Bernabéu.
Ronaldinho scored twice, and the Madrid crowd stood up and applauded a Barcelona player. Before him, only Diego Maradona (1983) had received that from the Bernabéu.

Which away player deserves a Bernabéu standing ovation today?
