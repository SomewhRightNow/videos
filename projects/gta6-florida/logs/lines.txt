In four days, GTA 6 is finally here. And its map is hiding a real place.
Rockstar's state of Leonida is its take on Florida.
Vice City is Miami. Same skyline, same beaches, same neon.
The Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys, islands chained into the ocean.
Grassrivers is the Everglades. Swamps, airboats, and alligators.
Ambrosia mirrors the sugar towns south of Lake Okeechobee.
Port Gellhorn looks a lot like the Gulf Coast.
But Mount Kalaga? Florida has no mountains. Rockstar built one.
Leading it all: Jason and Lucia.
The first trailer hit ninety million YouTube views in a single day.
