Facts: Singapore banned the import and sale of chewing gum in 1992 (therapeutic gum allowed since 2004); Longyearbyen's cemetery stopped
taking burials around 1950 because bodies do not decompose in the permafrost; Greece banned high heels at ancient sites in 2009;
Venice banned feeding pigeons in St Mark's Square in 2008; in Germany, stopping on the Autobahn because you ran out of fuel is a finable
offence; Switzerland's 2008 animal welfare rules require social animals like guinea pigs to be kept with a companion.
