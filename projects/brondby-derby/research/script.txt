Copenhagen, today. The derby hadn't even started.
As the players walked out, the Brøndby away end exploded. Rockets, flares, and smoke covered the pitch.
The referee sent the players back. Kickoff was delayed by five minutes.
It was Brøndby's first time back in the away end in over two years.
On the pitch, Copenhagen won one-nil, and went top of the league.
Now Brøndby could see their own stand closed for the next home game.
