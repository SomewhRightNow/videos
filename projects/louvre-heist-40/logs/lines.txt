Seven minutes. That's all it took to rob the Louvre.
Paris. Sunday, nine thirty in the morning.
The museum had been open for thirty minutes.
A furniture lift pulled up on the riverside.
Two men in yellow vests rode it up, and cut the glass.
Inside: the French Crown Jewels.
They smashed two display cases.
Eight pieces, worn by empresses.
Worth one hundred and two million dollars.
Four minutes later, they escaped on scooters.
Seven people arrested.
A year later, the jewels have never been found.
In nineteen eleven, the Mona Lisa vanished from the same building.
