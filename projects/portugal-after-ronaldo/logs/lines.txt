One hundred and forty-six goals. Two hundred and thirty-four games. Twenty-three years. And now, it's over.
On November fifth, Cristiano Ronaldo retired from the Portugal national team.
It came after a falling-out with coach Jorge Jesus, and a thirty-day suspension.
The boy from Madeira leaves as the top scorer in men's international football history.
But can Portugal win without him?
They already did once. In the Euro twenty sixteen final, Ronaldo went off injured after twenty-five minutes.
Portugal still beat France, one-nil, in extra time.
And a new generation is ready: Bruno Fernandes, Bernardo Silva, Vitinha, João Neves, Rafael Leão.
Their first test without him: Denmark, this Saturday, in Braga.
