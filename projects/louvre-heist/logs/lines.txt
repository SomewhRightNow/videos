Seven minutes. That's all it took to rob the most famous museum on Earth.
Paris. Sunday, October nineteenth, twenty twenty-five. Nine thirty in the morning.
The Louvre had been open for just thirty minutes.
A truck with a furniture lift pulled up on the riverside.
Two men in yellow vests rode it up to a window, and cut through the glass.
Behind it, the Apollo Gallery. Home of the French Crown Jewels.
They smashed two display cases.
Eight pieces, worn by empresses and queens.
Worth one hundred and two million dollars.
Less than four minutes inside. Then they escaped on scooters.
Seven people have been arrested.
But a year later, the jewels have never been found.
And it wasn't the first time. In nineteen eleven, the Mona Lisa vanished from the same building.
