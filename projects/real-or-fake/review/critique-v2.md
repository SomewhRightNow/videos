# Critique: draft-v2.mp4

1. **4.8 s–52 s · The twist is given away by round 2.** Every round is stamped REAL straight away, and the dots at the top fill with green ticks. By #2 or #3 the viewer has worked out that every answer is REAL. So the quiz has no tension, and "All five are real" at 52.68 s tells them nothing new. This is the main reason a viewer would swipe away around 25–30 s. **Smallest fix:** keep the countdown, but after it lock the answer as a neutral "?" or "LOCKED IN". Keep the place name and the fact, and save every green REAL stamp for the 52.68 s reveal. The per-round narration also has to lose the word "Real.": "Salar de Uyuni, Bolivia…".

2. **0.0–4.8 s · The opening banner labels the wrong photos.** The cold open shows four of the five photos (tunnel, geyser, eucalyptus, car on the salt flat), and the narration says "number five is the hardest". But the "#5 IS THE HARDEST" banner sits on top of the Tunnel of Love, Fly Geyser and the salt flat as well, not only on the eucalyptus. A first-time viewer reads each of those as "#5" and is then confused when #1 turns out to be the same salt flat they were just told is #5. **Smallest fix:** show the banner only over the eucalyptus shot (about 2.5–3.3 s). Or swap it for a neutral "5 PHOTOS" tag while the other photos are on screen.

3. **35.1–41.96 s · A 7-second still hold on Fly Geyser.** The REAL stamp, the place name and the caption all stay locked after the 1964 line ends, with no new information. It is the longest dead stretch in the film, and it sits right before the "hardest" round, which is the pay-off. Swipe risk. **Smallest fix:** shorten the hold by about 3 s and start "Number five" sooner. Or add a slow push-in or a cut to a second angle of the geyser.
