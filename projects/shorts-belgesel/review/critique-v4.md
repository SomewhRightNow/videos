# Critique · draft-v4 (540x960, 30.21 s, 24 fps)

## 1. 23.54–30.2 · the ending is one still that is not about him, held 6.7 s
What a viewer sees: after the cut at 23.54 the whole last fifth of the film is one framed engraving tagged "MÉLIÈS · STAGED ERUPTION · 1902" (sheet 24–30; strip-26s). The narration says "he tours with a circus", but there is no circus and no Sylbaris: we last see the man at 23.5 and the film ends on a volcano picture from someone else's film. From 24 to 26.2 nothing new happens (only a slow push; the typewriter title starts at about 26.2). A first-time viewer reads the Méliès tag as a caption error, and the payoff ("the man who lived") is shown over the volcano, not over the man.
Smallest fix: keep the Sylbaris portrait (or a circus poster/billing of him) as the picture for 23.9–30; if the Méliès still stays, cut it to 1.5 s at most (23.5–25) and then go back to his face for the title. Start the title typing at 25.0 instead of 26.2, so 24–26 is not dead.

## 2. 20.4–23.54 · the day counter stops at DAY 3 while the voice says four days
What a viewer sees: "DAY 2" (21.7–22.6), "DAY 3" (22.6–23.5), then a hard cut to the Méliès still (strip-22s). There is no DAY 1 and no DAY 4, so the count visibly falls short of "four days" and the cut lands mid-count; the burns on his back are also on screen only 2.5 s.
Smallest fix: run DAY 1 → DAY 4 at about 0.6 s a step from 20.8, landing DAY 4 by 23.2, and hold it 0.3 s before the cut at 23.54 (or move the cut to 23.9, where the next line starts).

## 3. 18.0–20.4 · the window diagram says the opposite of the narration
What a viewer sees: a box labelled "WINDOW" with an arrow labelled "PELÉE" pointing straight at it from the right (sheet 19–20; strip-19s). Read cold, that says the mountain faces the window, while the voice says "its window turned away from the mountain". The box also floats over ivy above the cell, not on the cell itself.
Smallest fix: put the PELÉE arrow on the side opposite the window (the window on the left wall, Pelée off the right edge behind the solid back wall), or make the window face away with a small wall bar between them. Move the box down onto the cell's front wall by about 120 px at this size.
