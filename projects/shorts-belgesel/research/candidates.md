# Candidates (30 s Shorts documentary, real stock + archive, fog, faded film, grain)

1. **The comet that came apart** (spark s3, seed: Comet 73P "stepping stones"). 1995 it split; by 2006 NASA counted dozens of pieces trailing in a line; in 2022 Earth flew through its dust waiting for a meteor storm. Images: NASA/Hubble/Spitzer frames of the fragment chain (public domain), observatory archive, foggy night sky stock. Form: a slow countdown of pieces, 1 → 3 → 60+.
2. **The stone that rings** (spark s1, seed: Gobustan). Gaval Daş, a rock in Azerbaijan that rings like a drum when struck, beside 6,000 carved figures and steaming mud volcanoes; people may have played it for thousands of years. Images: petroglyph photos, mud volcano fog, hands striking stone. Form: told by its sound, each strike a cut.
3. **The prisoner the volcano missed.** Mount Pelée, 8 May 1902: Saint-Pierre gone in about a minute; one of very few survivors, Ludger Sylbaris, was in a thick-walled cell. Images: 1902 archive photos of the ruined city, ash clouds, a dungeon wall. Form: one minute of one morning, told from inside the cell.
4. **The bridge rebuilt from photographs** (spark s2, seed: Konjic). Konjic's Old Bridge, blown up in 1945, rebuilt in 2009 stone by stone from old photos. Images: archive photos of the bridge, the river in mist, the new arches. Form: then and now, the photo dissolving into the stone.
5. **The island that was never there.** Sandy Island sat on charts and Google Earth for over a century; in 2012 a research ship sailed to it and found 1,400 metres of open ocean. Images: old nautical charts, sea fog, a ship's sonar line. Form: an investigation that ends on empty water.
